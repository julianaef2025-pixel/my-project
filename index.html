What is it?

AI Engineering defined

AI Engineering bridges software engineering, data science, and machine learning operations. It is the discipline of building production-grade AI systems — not just research prototypes, but reliable, maintainable, and scalable applications powered by intelligent models.

🧠

Model-Centric

Design and fine-tune large language models, vision transformers, and domain-specific architectures to solve real problems.

🔗

Systems Thinking

Connect models to data pipelines, APIs, vector stores, and feedback loops that keep systems accurate over time.

📦

Production Ready

Ship models as low-latency services with monitoring, versioning, rollback, and CI/CD built in from day one.

Core disciplines

The Four Pillars

01
📊

Data Engineering

Curate, clean, and version datasets. Build feature stores, streaming pipelines, and annotation workflows that feed hungry models.

  • ETL & ELT pipelines
  • Feature stores
  • Data versioning (DVC)
  • Synthetic data generation
02
🔬

Model Development

Train, evaluate, and iterate on models. From classical ML to foundation model fine-tuning, experimentation is at the core.

  • Supervised & unsupervised learning
  • Fine-tuning & RLHF
  • Hyperparameter search
  • Experiment tracking (MLflow)
03
🚀

MLOps & Deployment

Operationalize models at scale. Containerise, serve, monitor, and continuously improve them in production environments.

  • Model serving (Triton, TorchServe)
  • Docker & Kubernetes
  • A/B testing & canary deploys
  • Drift detection
04
🛡

AI Safety & Ethics

Build systems people can trust. Apply interpretability methods, fairness audits, and responsible-AI frameworks by default.

  • Model explainability (SHAP, LIME)
  • Bias & fairness auditing
  • Red-teaming & adversarial testing
  • Governance & compliance

Learning path

Your Roadmap

Phase 1 — Foundations

Math, Code & Data

Linear algebra, calculus, probability, Python fluency, pandas, NumPy, and SQL. Everything downstream relies on these.

Phase 2 — Core ML

Classical Machine Learning

Regression, classification, clustering, and ensemble methods. Understand the bias-variance tradeoff and model evaluation rigorously.

Phase 3 — Deep Learning

Neural Networks & Modern Architectures

CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, and attention. Build and train models with PyTorch. Understand backpropagation from scratch.

Phase 4 — Specialise

NLP, Vision, or Reinforcement Learning

Go deep in one domain. Fine-tune LLMs, build RAG systems, train vision models, or design RL environments.

Phase 5 — Production

MLOps & Systems Design

Deploy with confidence. Learn model serving, monitoring, retraining pipelines, and cost-optimised inference.
