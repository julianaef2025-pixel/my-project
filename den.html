Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay to use DEN?
No. The Free plan gives you 50 messages a day with no credit card required. You just need a free Groq API key from console.groq.com.
What is DEN Code?
DEN Code is a specialized AI mode built specifically for software engineering. It writes clean, production-ready code, debugs problems, writes unit tests, reviews for security vulnerabilities, and explains code in any language.
What AI model powers DEN?
DEN uses Llama 3.3 70B running on Groq infrastructure — one of the fastest and most capable open models available. Pro users get priority access during peak times.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel your Pro or Enterprise subscription at any time, no questions asked. You'll keep full access until the end of your current billing period.
Is my data private?
Your API key is stored only in your browser and never sent to our servers. Your conversations go directly from your browser to Groq's API.
